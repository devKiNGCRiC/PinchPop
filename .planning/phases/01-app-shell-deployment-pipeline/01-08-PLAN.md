## Typography role to Tailwind class map (Revision 3)

`DESIGN.md` section 4 type table; `01-UI-SPEC.md` Typography table. Display is 56px desktop / 34px
mobile — **not** 60/36.

| Role | Classes |
|---|---|
| Display | `font-heading text-[34px] leading-[1.05] font-bold md:text-[56px]` |
| Heading | `font-heading text-[22px] leading-[1.15] font-bold md:text-[30px]` |
| Body | `font-sans text-base leading-[1.6] font-normal` |
| Body (emphasis / button label) | `font-sans text-base leading-[1.6] font-semibold` |
| Label (HUD/nav/footer) | `font-mono text-[13px] leading-[1.4] font-normal tracking-[0.08em] uppercase` |

Exactly three weight values exist system-wide: Space Grotesk 700, Inter 400 and 600, IBM Plex Mono
400. In hand-written source that means:

- `font-heading` is always paired with `font-bold`, never `font-normal` or `font-semibold`.
- `font-sans` is paired with `font-normal` (body) or `font-semibold` (emphasis and button labels only).
- `font-mono` is always paired with `font-normal` — never `font-semibold`, never `font-bold`.
- `font-medium`, `font-light`, `font-thin`, `font-extralight`, `font-extrabold`, and `font-black` are
  forbidden outright — they would synthesize against font files that were never loaded.

The shadcn-generated files under `src/components/ui/` are exempt and **must not be edited** — the
`button.tsx` base class list ships `font-medium` and `text-sm`, both of which are overridden at the
call site by `font-semibold text-base` (tailwind-merge dedupes the conflicting utilities).

IBM Plex Mono is restricted to the Label/HUD role — nav links, the wordmark, and the footer line.
It must not appear on body copy, headings, or generic emphasis (`DESIGN.md` section 4, section 21).

## Shared press-feedback recipe

`DESIGN.md` section 8 gives every button variant the same scale-on-press feedback; only the glow is
primary-exclusive. `DESIGN.md` section 19 requires all of it to collapse under reduced motion. Both
CTAs therefore share this base:

`transition-all duration-200 ease-out hover:scale-[1.03] active:scale-[0.97] motion-reduce:transition-none motion-reduce:hover:scale-100 motion-reduce:active:scale-100`

The primary CTA additionally layers on `hover:shadow-lg hover:shadow-shutter/25`. The 404 secondary
CTA does not — `DESIGN.md` section 8 makes the secondary treatment explicitly shadowless. Do not add
the glow to the 404 button, and do not omit the scale or `motion-reduce:` classes from it.

Every interactive element also carries
`focus-visible:outline-2 focus-visible:outline-offset-2 focus-visible:outline-shutter`
(`DESIGN.md` section 18: 2px shutter ring on every interactive element, no exceptions, never suppressed).

## Two documented reconciliations the executor must not "fix"

Both are resolved in favor of `DESIGN.md` and are called out here so a reviewer does not read them as
oversights.

1. **`PlaceholderCard` gets no hover lift or rotate.** `01-UI-SPEC.md`'s Component Styling Approach
   describes a lift-plus-rotate hover on cards. `DESIGN.md` section 9 scopes that treatment to
   info/stat cards that are "itself interactive (clickable), not... static stat tiles." The five stub
   cards are static, non-clickable informational surfaces, so applying a lift would falsely signal
   clickability. `DESIGN.md` wins: the card is flat, with a soft ambient shadow and no hover
   transform. This also means the card needs no `motion-reduce:` variant, because it has no motion.
2. **The nav wordmark stays IBM Plex Mono uppercase, not Space Grotesk.** `DESIGN.md` section 7 says
   the wordmark is Space Grotesk bold; `01-UI-SPEC.md` Revision 3 ran an explicit compatibility audit
   of the nav against `DESIGN.md` section 7 and deliberately kept the mono Label treatment, citing
   section 4's inclusion of nav labels in the mono role. Revision 3 is the approved, post-audit
   document, so the mono wordmark ships. **The Task 5 checkpoint explicitly surfaces this to the
   developer for a decision** rather than silently resolving it — do not change it before then.
