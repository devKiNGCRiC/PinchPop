## Decorative color accessibility — hard rule

`01-UI-SPEC.md` states: any time a Decorative Motif color is used as a solid fill large enough to
contain text or an icon, its foreground must be Ink (`#1D1626`), never white or cream. This plan
enforces that **by construction**: every route icon renders as an Ink-stroked glyph inside a
color-filled chip, so no per-instance contrast check is ever needed. Never render a Decorative Motif
color as a bare stroke or text color on a Cream or Paper White surface — Flash Lime especially is
effectively invisible that way.
